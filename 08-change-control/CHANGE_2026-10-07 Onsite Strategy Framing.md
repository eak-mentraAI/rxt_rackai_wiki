# 2026-10-07 — Onsite Strategy Framing

## Trigger

Preparing the leadership onsite (RackAI roadmap + MI350P benchmarking), the product owner set three strategic framings, now carried into the canonical notes so the deck, briefs and wiki tell one story.

## Objects Changed

- **[[Three Battlegrounds]]**: sovereignty elevated to a **co-equal centre of gravity** with operations. New *Two centres of gravity* section: Operator ("we take responsibility for running it") and Sovereign provider ("it stays theirs, and they can verify it"); a positioning model, framed as the intersection we target rather than a market map; a note that infrastructure qualification evidences only part of the sovereign half. The single identity, Private Enterprise AI Operator, is kept; its two halves are the two centres. The earlier line calling sovereignty purely a delivery mechanism is revised and the change is stated explicitly.
- **[[RackAI Roadmap]]** sequencing logic: AI co-development has cut the cost of code, so we plan in **six-month horizons** and expect to rebuild. Principle 1 (irreversibility) now means *what code can't regenerate*: uncaptured operating and cost data, customer trust, isolation and sovereignty boundaries, and compliance lead times. The placement-interface rationale is now optionality plus the adopted product principle, not retrofit cost. P-003 / P-004 class labels and the one-line exec answer are updated.
- **[[Minimum Operable Estate]]**: new section on what MOE-1 changes (supplier to sovereign operator) and a **proposed acceptance definition** of five artifacts, with a new open question to ratify them.
