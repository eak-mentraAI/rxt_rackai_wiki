**Product Manager, AI Assurance and Governance**
Product Management · Remote / Hybrid

**The opportunity**

RackAI is Rackspace's managed AI inference platform, built for the private and sovereign environments where regulated enterprises can actually run AI. What makes that claim defensible is not the serving layer but the controls around it: policy enforced as the system runs, and a record afterwards of what happened and on whose authority. That is the product that earns the enterprise's trust — and the multiples that come with it.

Enterprises have stopped asking whether AI can work and started asking who is accountable when it gets something wrong. Turning that question into a product — controls a customer can rely on and an auditor will accept — is one of the most valuable and least-crowded areas in enterprise AI, and the regulatory timetable has made it urgent.

As Product Manager, AI Assurance and Governance you own the product direction for that trust layer: runtime policy, provenance and replay, agent identity, and the certification evidence the platform rests on. You decide what we build and why; a Lead Governance Engineer and specialist engineers own how. It is a founding product seat where the outcome — a governed platform regulated buyers adopt — is yours to define and the path yours to choose.

**What you'll do**

- Own the roadmap for the governance controls built into the first real workload — runtime policy enforcement, the provenance-and-replay record every later claim is checked against, and tenant isolation — proven on a live deployment rather than documented.
- Own the agent identity and delegated-authority product: service identities, scoped tokens, and permissions that expire when a task ends rather than being inherited whole — the layer that sits upstream of action controls and information-flow control.
- Own the requirements for verification and audit evidence so that, over time, an audit becomes a query against the platform — working the seam with the harness owner, since the same per-step checks serve as reliability signal and audit evidence.
- Own the compliance-envelope roadmap: the SOC 2 and sovereign attestations that gate whether a regulated buyer can adopt at all, sequenced against their long lead times and paired with a named certification owner.
- Own the product side of the multi-cluster and residency path — the governance that lets a tenant's traffic and placement stay in approved regions as we expand — including the Avi/Broadcom gateway evaluation that closes the global-front-door gap.
- Hold the honest line between what is shipped (baseline enforcement), what is in flight (certification, provenance), and what is research (trustworthy per-step verification, governable self-modification), so customers and leadership trust the sovereign claim.

**What you'll bring**

- Eight or more years in product management, including a product area in security, identity, governance, compliance or a regulated domain.
- A working grasp of how AI or platform controls are enforced and evidenced — policy-as-code, identity and access, provenance and audit — as product concerns, not just concepts.
- A track record turning regulatory and customer requirements into a shipped roadmap that engineers can build against.
- Fluency working with senior security and platform engineers as peers while owning the "what and why."
- Direct experience delivering inside regulated, audited or sovereign environments, and familiarity with what an auditor accepts as evidence.
- Familiarity with control frameworks — SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001 or 42001, NIST AI RMF — as things a product is measured against.

**Nice to have**

- Experience taking a product through a certification audit.
- Exposure to LLM-based or agentic systems and their specific governance risks — prompt injection, delegated authority, information-flow control.
- A background bridging product with compliance, risk or legal.
