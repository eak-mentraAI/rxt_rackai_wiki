**Product Manager, AI Orchestration and Harness**
Product Management · Remote / Hybrid

**The opportunity**

RackAI is Rackspace's managed AI inference platform, built for the private and sovereign environments where regulated enterprises can actually run AI. Serving a model reliably, though, is not the same as running an agentic system a customer can put into production. The harness — the context, tools, memory and guardrails around a model — and the orchestration above it are what turn a raw model into dependable work.

An enterprise does not run one agent; it runs many, one per domain, each with its own tools and policy. Deciding which of them handles a task, on which model, at what cost, and when to involve a person, is where the product either earns its keep or doesn't. Very few teams are shaping that as a product on evidence rather than guesswork, and that is the gap this role owns.

As Product Manager, AI Orchestration and Harness you own the product direction for that layer. You decide what we build and why, against real customers and real workloads, and you partner with a Chief Architect and Principal Engineer who own how it is built. It is a founding product seat: the problems, the priorities and the definition of done are yours to set.

**What you'll do**

- Own the roadmap for the governed harness — the context, tools, memory and guardrails around a model — and define what the repeatable harness pattern must do to be trusted, proven first on one real customer workload before it repeats across domains.
- Own the product requirements for the durable runtime: replay and recovery of an agent run, observable traces, and cost-per-outcome measured from the first run rather than bolted on later.
- Own the orchestration and routing product — sending each task to the right harness, model or human, choosing the cheapest executor that clears a reliability bar — and decide where simple rules end and learned optimization earns its place.
- Turn the empirical map — where each model is reliable, on which workload, at what cost, from our own operating data rather than public benchmarks — into a product surface that routing reads and internal teams trust.
- Define how the harness carries agent identity and delegated authority so a run acts under scoped, revocable permissions, working with the assurance-and-governance owner on the seam.
- Decide the sequencing from a single proven workload to a harness other teams build on, and own the trade-offs — what ships now versus what waits on the research tail (long-horizon memory, learned planning).

**What you'll bring**

- Eight or more years in product management, including time owning a technical platform or developer-facing product end to end.
- Direct experience with LLM-based or agentic systems — orchestration, tool use, context assembly, or evaluation — as a product area, not a side interest.
- A track record turning an ambiguous, fast-moving problem into a clear roadmap and shipped outcomes.
- Fluency in working with architects and senior engineers as peers, holding the "what and why" while they hold the "how."
- Comfort making decisions from measurement — cost, reliability, workload data — rather than opinion.
- Experience delivering inside regulated, audited or sovereign environments, or the judgment to operate credibly there.

**Nice to have**

- Experience with an internal developer platform, SDK, or agent framework with real consumers.
- A background in inference economics or cost-per-outcome thinking as a product constraint.
- Prior founding-PM experience standing up a new product area.
