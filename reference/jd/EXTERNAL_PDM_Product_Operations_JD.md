**Product Operations Lead**
Product Operations · Remote / Hybrid

**The opportunity**

RackAI is Rackspace's managed AI inference platform, built for the private and sovereign environments where regulated enterprises can actually run AI. Building capabilities is one thing; turning them into offers that can be sold, provisioned, priced, supported and operated is another — and it is the difference between a demo and a business.

RackAI is assembling fast: serving, harness, orchestration, optimization, assurance and governance, each with its own product owner and roadmap. Someone has to make those pieces land as coherent offers — to work across the product, engineering, field, finance and operations teams so that what we build actually reaches customers and runs. That connective, operational product work is what this role owns, and it is what keeps a fast-moving platform from shipping capabilities no one can buy.

This is an operations role, not a product-management one — the distinction that matters here is internal-facing versus external-facing. The external product managers own what we build for customers and why. You own how those pieces come together and run inside the company. You work across teams rather than owning a single product layer, and the outcomes — offers that ship cleanly and operate reliably — are yours; how you orchestrate the teams to get there is your call.

**What you'll do**

- Operationalize the RackAI solution set as it ships — model garden, fine-tuning, serving, the console experience, and the routes to market on top of it, including becoming a public and private provider on OpenRouter — coordinating the product, engineering, capacity and field teams so each offer goes live cleanly rather than as a scramble.
- Stand up the launch-readiness discipline that repeats for every new model, capability and offer: the gates across conformance, capacity provisioning, metering, pricing inputs and support that decide whether it is genuinely ready to sell and run — whether it reaches customers directly, through the app, or via a distribution channel.
- Wire the metering-and-showback path across teams — usage capture in the serving path, attribution by tenant, and the cost reporting that FinOps and the field depend on — so an offer is accountable from day one.
- Coordinate capacity-pool provisioning and reserve/warm/canary allocation with the optimization and infrastructure teams as new models and tenants come online.
- Assemble the certification-and-compliance evidence across the governance, engineering and security teams into a repeatable package, so an audit is a process we run, not a fire drill.
- Turn recurring cross-team friction — the same handoffs that stall every launch — into standing runbooks, so the next offer ships faster than the last.

**What you'll bring**

- Eight or more years in product operations, technical program management, or launch/release operations, including cross-functional delivery of complex products.
- A track record turning capabilities into launched, operable offers — packaging, pricing inputs, provisioning, support and go-live.
- Strong operational instincts: you build the process that's missing rather than waiting for one.
- The ability to work across product, engineering, field and finance as peers and hold many moving parts to a coherent plan.
- Comfort in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment where the org and the offers are still forming.
- Experience delivering inside regulated, audited or sovereign environments, or the judgment to operate credibly there.

**Nice to have**

- Experience operationalizing offers for a platform or infrastructure product.
- Familiarity with AI/ML or inference products and their provisioning and support realities.
- A background bridging product and go-to-market or revenue operations.
